# AI Video Production Planner

A simple browser-based app that takes text, topic, or extracted PDF content and generates a complete AI video production plan.

## Usage

1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Choose the input type.
3. Paste your content or topic.
4. Click "Generate Video Plan".

The app outputs structured :

- Title and summary
- 5 scenes with narration, visuals, image prompts, and emotion
- A combined voiceover script
- Avatar instructions
- Video assembly steps
